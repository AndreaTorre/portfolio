The material was produced during the Artificial Intelligence course of the Master’s Degree in Computer Science at the University of Milano-Bicocca.

It includes a presentation on “To Believe or Not to Believe,” in which the article was analyzed and personally presented during the lectures.

The remaining material concerns the final project.
I worked on the development and evaluation of a closed-domain question-
answering pipeline based on complex legal documents. The system combined semantic
retrieval through sentence embeddings and FAISS with lexical retrieval based on
BM25, allowing us to compare dense, sparse and hybrid retrieval strategies. We
evaluated two prompting approaches using RAGAS metrics and complemented the
quantitative analysis with a manual examination of unsupported claims. This project
gave me practical experience with information retrieval, language models, prompt
design and model evaluation, while also teaching me to critically assess the reliability
and limitations of automated evaluation methods.
